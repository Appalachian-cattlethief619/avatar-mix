## 🎯 About This Tool

Avatar-mix helps you build videos for social media. It combines your HeyGen avatar with animated backgrounds, custom music, and sound effects. The app also adds dynamic captions in the style popularized by Alex Hormozi. You create 16:9 widescreen videos or 9:16 vertical clips for platforms like TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts. This tool automates the technical steps of video editing so you focus on your content.

## ⚙️ System Requirements

- 8GB of RAM or more
- 500MB of free disk space
- An active internet connection

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Usage

### Preparing Your Content
Before you start the software, gather your assets. You need your HeyGen avatar export, the background video files, and your audio tracks. Keep these files in a single folder on your desktop for easy access.

### Setting Up Your Project
1. Open avatar-mix.
2. Select the '+' button to start a new project.
3. Choose your video format. Select 16:9 for YouTube or 9:16 for vertical platforms.
4. Import your HeyGen avatar file.

### Adding Backgrounds and Sound
The interface displays a timeline. Drag your HyperFrame background files into the background track. Place your avatar file on the top track so it sits over the background. Use the audio import section to add music and sound effects. Adjust the volume sliders to get the right mix between your voice and the music.

### Generating Captions
The app uses speech-to-text to transcribe your video. Select the 'Generate Captions' button. The software creates text overlays during the process. Review the text in the editing window to ensure accuracy. If you notice a typo, click the text block to type the correction. You can change the font, color, and size of the letters in the style panel on the right side of the screen.

### Rendering Your Video
Once you finish the edit, select the 'Export' button. Choose your output folder. The software encodes the video file. This process uses your computer processor to combine the background, avatar, and text. Higher quality settings result in larger file sizes but sharper images.

## 🔍 Frequently Asked Questions

### Does the app require a monthly fee?
The application is free to download and use. You may need a separate subscription to HeyGen for your avatar assets.

### Can I save my work?
The app saves your project file automatically. You can close the program and return later to finish your edit.

### Where do I find my finished videos?
Files appear in the 'Videos' folder under your Documents library unless you choose a different location during export.

### Is my content private?
Your files stay on your computer. You choose when and where to upload your finished videos.

## 📋 Troubleshooting Tips

- If the app fails to start, check that your Windows system has the latest updates.
- If you notice stuttering during playback, close other heavy programs such as web browsers or photo editing tools.
- If the audio does not sync, ensure your audio files are in a standard format like MP3 or WAV. 
- If the rendering process stops, verify that you have enough space on your hard drive. 

## 🌐 Managing Your Social Media Workflow
Once you have your final video file, you are ready to post. Use the native apps on your phone or your computer browser to upload the content directly to your social network. The file formats created by avatar-mix are compatible with all major platforms. If you encounter issues during the upload process, ensure your internet connection is stable. Remember to add tags and descriptions relevant to your specific niche to help social media algorithms index your video correctly.
